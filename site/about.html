World Desk

About this briefing

World Desk is a free, automated collection of links to reporting published elsewhere. It helps readers discover stories and visit the original reporting.

How stories are selected

The daily collector reads configured RSS feeds and searches the GDELT news index. It removes repeated links and headlines and assembles up to 50 story leads across the sources available at publication time. Source outages can make an issue much smaller or narrower in coverage.

The current RSS feed is Hacker News, a technology-focused community feed. GDELT searches cover broader subjects including science, health, world affairs, culture, sport, and business. Coverage is incomplete and source listings may contain mistakes.

Reading the original report

Each story identifies and links to its source. Publishers retain rights to their reporting and images. World Desk does not reproduce full articles or source photographs. A linked publisher may require its own subscription.

Automation and accuracy

Issues are assembled automatically and are not independently fact-checked by an editor. The current public format contains headlines and source links. Publication metadata may be incomplete; lack of a U.S. search match does not establish that a story is new to U.S. readers.

Follow the source link for full context, updates, and corrections. Use authoritative primary sources for consequential health, financial, legal, or safety decisions.

Free access

World Desk does not charge readers or require a reader account. Advertising is not currently active. Any future sponsored material will be identified as advertising or sponsorship.
